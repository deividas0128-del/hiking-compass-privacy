Privacy Policy for Hiking Compass

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Hiking Compass (package lt.zygiai.kompasas).

1. Summary

Hiking Compass is designed to work primarily on your device. The app does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics, and does not sell personal data.

2. Location data

With your permission, Hiking Compass may access approximate and/or precise device location while you are using the app. Location is used to provide hiking and navigation features such as coordinates, altitude, speed, GPS movement bearing, and calculations related to true north.

Location data is processed on your device for these features. It is not sent to the developer, sold, or shared with third parties by the app. The app does not request background location access.

3. Device sensor data

The app uses device sensors such as the rotation vector, accelerometer, magnetometer, and related motion/orientation sensors to calculate compass direction. Sensor readings are processed locally on the device and are not transmitted to the developer.

4. Local preferences

The app may store basic preferences on your device, such as selected bearing, north mode, or visual theme. These settings remain on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

5. Data collection, sharing, and sale

The developer does not collect, store on external servers, sell, rent, or share personal information through Hiking Compass. The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions

Location permission is requested only to provide location-dependent navigation information. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings. Core compass functionality may still be available using device sensors, while GPS-related features may be unavailable.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the developer does not collect personal data on external servers, there is no developer-held personal data to retain or delete. Any locally stored app settings can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Hiking Compass.

8. Children's privacy

Hiking Compass is a general-purpose navigation utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users.

9. Security

The app minimizes data handling by processing navigation and sensor information locally on the device. Users should still keep their Android device and operating system updated and use normal device security protections.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
